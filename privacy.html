This page explains how jinjagames.com handles information from visitors.

Information we collect

We do not ask visitors to create an account or submit a form on this website. If you email us, we receive the information you include in that email.

Fonts and third parties

The typefaces used on this site are served from this domain. No fonts, scripts, analytics or embeds are loaded from a third party, so your visit is not reported to anyone.

Local preferences

The website stores your selected language, theme and intro preference in your browser's local storage. These settings stay on your device and are not sent to JinjaGames.

External links

Steam, Instagram, LinkedIn and email links take you to third party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, email jinjagamesofficial@gmail.com.
