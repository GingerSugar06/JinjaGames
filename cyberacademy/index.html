What it is

Two sides, one game.

The academy

Twenty five hands on modules teach real cybersecurity: phishing, passwords, networks, malware, incident response, cryptography and more. You do not read about it, you do it. Every concept is a puzzle you solve inside a realistic tool, so the knowledge sticks.

The dark web

Then the training goes live. Run your own virtual machine, scan targets, breach walls with tool chains, steal credits, plant keyloggers, and trace intruders. Build firewalls, honeypots and defenses. A real economy with honest rules, where every attack and defense has a cost.

Inside the game

Deep systems, real depth.

No fake terminals. No hand holding. Master the tools and the mind games.

Attack chains

Scan, forge, breach and extract with signal chain tools. Each step is a puzzle.

Defense apps

Firewalls, SIEM, process guard, honeypots. Configure them through mini puzzles.

Keyloggers and traces

Plant implants, read what targets type, and hunt attackers back.

Living economy

Bank, market, contracts and heists. Grow your credits, defend your vault.

Skill tree

Attack power, stealth, defense, repair. Shape your operator across branches.

CyberCode editor

Write scripts in a fictional DSL, with syntax highlight and autocomplete.
